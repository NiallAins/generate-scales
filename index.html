Generating scales

An Octave is created by picking a base frequency and multiplying by 2.

A seven note Diatonic Scale can be created by taking a base frequency and then repeatedly multiplying it by 1.5 (a ratio of 3/2, a Perfect Fifth interval) to generate seven notes.
If a note goes beyond the Octave, it is divided by 2 to bring it back inside the Octave.

The Minor Scale and Major Scale can be found by looping through these seven notes, starting on the third and fifth notes respectively.

The notes are named using letters, with the letter A given to the first note of the Minor Scale.
As a result, our original base frequency ends up as F.

The full twleve note Chromantic Scale can be created by continuing to multiply by 1.5 to generate five additional notes.

This Chromatic Scale divides the Octave almost evenly into twelve — an Equal Temperment tuning is generated by dividing the octave exatly into twelve.
